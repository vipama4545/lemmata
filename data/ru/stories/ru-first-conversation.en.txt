A first conversation

Anna: Hello!
John: Hello. What is your name?
Anna: My name is Anna. And you?
John: My name is John.
Anna: Very nice to meet you. Where are you from?
John: I am from America. I am American. And are you Russian?
Anna: Yes, I am Russian. I am from Moscow.
John: Very nice to meet you.
Anna: Goodbye!
John: Goodbye!
