The Tortoise and the Hare

"Look at yourself!" said the hare. "It takes you the whole morning just to cross the field!"

One day, the tortoise grew tired of this teasing, calmly raised his head, and told him: "I may be slow, but I will beat you in a race."

The hare thought this was a joke and gladly agreed. They asked the wise fox to be the judge.

When the race started, the hare ran very fast and left the tortoise far behind. After going a short distance, the hare thought, "The tortoise will never catch up to me." He lay down on the green grass and soon fell asleep.

Meanwhile, the tortoise kept moving forward slowly. He never stopped to rest. Hours passed while the hare slept, and the tortoise quietly passed him, getting very close to the finish line.

When the hare woke up, the sun was already low. He realized that he had slept too long. The hare ran quickly toward the finish line, but someone had beaten him to it. He saw that the tortoise had already crossed the finish line. The forest animals were shouting with joy and cheering for the tortoise.
